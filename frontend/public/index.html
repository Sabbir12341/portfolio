About Me

Get to know who I am and what drives my passion

I'm an AI Automation Engineer at Smartdata Technology Ltd in Dhaka, Bangladesh. I built the automation behind OmniPost, a multi-brand AI customer messaging platform that is live for 3 brands: n8n workflows, AI agents that classify, draft and check replies, and a TypeScript engine built with Claude Code. It is all connected to Meta, Google, Telegram and OpenRouter through APIs and webhooks.

I build for accuracy: an audit trail for every change, access control per client, cost tracking for every AI call, and planned-vs-actual reconciliation reports. I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering, and my research on human activity recognition has been accepted at IEEE COMPAS 2026.

Next, I want to apply these strengths to automating financial workflows such as data entry, reconciliation and reporting. I'm based in Dhaka, open to remote work during US business hours, and I work in Bangla and English.

  • Built the end-to-end AI pipeline (intake → classification → drafting → decision routing → publishing) as n8n workflows for Facebook, Messenger, Instagram, LinkedIn and Google reviews. Later moved the Meta part into a TypeScript engine, so a message in manual mode now uses no n8n execution.
  • Developed the client dashboard with Claude Code (Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase/PostgreSQL): 40+ versioned SQL migrations, row-level security so each business sees only its own data (with automated tests that prove it), and an audit log recording who changed what, with before/after values.
  • Built an LLM reply agent that answers in English and Bangla from each brand's knowledge base (products, FAQs, owner rules) and the conversation history. Confidence-based routing sends unsure or sensitive cases to human approval.
  • Added a second-pass AI check that holds any reply that guesses or invents facts. In a replay of real customer questions it caught 15 of 15 at about $0.0005 per message.
  • Reduced AI cost: every AI call's tokens and cost are logged to a usage ledger. Sending only the products a message mentions cut a ~9,400-token prompt. Rule-based quick replies now answer the button taps that made up 72% of one client's messages, with no AI cost.
  • Document-to-data automation: owners upload Excel, CSV, Word or PDF files, and an LLM extracts structured products, prices and FAQs into the database. Existing records are matched and updated, not duplicated.
  • Reconciliation reporting: parses monthly content plans, syncs published posts from the Meta API every 6 hours, matches planned vs. actual items and produces green/amber/red weekly compliance scorecards.
  • Integrated the Meta Graph API with signature-verified (HMAC) webhooks, plus the Google Business Profile API, OpenRouter, Supabase and the Telegram Bot API (real-time reservation alerts to staff). Added an error-logging workflow and diagnosed live production issues from data.
